# Form Of Rail - Notes

This is a public research journal. Not a manifesto. Not a pitch deck. A journal.

## The Four Rails

An operation needs four rails to be complete:

- **input** — the data that arrives
- **output** — the data that leaves on success  
- **error** — the data that leaves on failure
- **trait** — what the operation is like

The operation took four years to find. The rails took four days. Because the operation was new — and the rails were always there.

## What we claim
